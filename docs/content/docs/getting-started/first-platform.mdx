---
title: "Connect the first platform"
description: "Connect a local or remote Docker Standalone or Swarm platform to Citadel."
---

1. Sign in as an administrator or an account allowed to create Platforms.
2. Open **Platforms** and select **Add**.
3. Choose Docker Standalone or Docker Swarm.
4. Choose the connector: local Docker, regular Agent, or Edge Agent.
5. Enter the connector settings shown by the form and save.
6. Wait for the health indicator and inventory counts to update.

For a local connector, Core must have access to the Docker socket. For a remote
connector, follow [Regular Agent](/docs/operations/agent) or
[Edge Agent](/docs/operations/edge-agent). A Swarm platform must connect to a
manager, and node-local functionality requires node-agent coverage on the
relevant Nodes.

The platform page reports connectivity separately from Swarm quorum. Resolve a
disconnected connector or unhealthy manager quorum before applying workloads.
