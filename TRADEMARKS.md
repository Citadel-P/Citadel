# Citadel name and logo

The Citadel name and logos identify the Docker management project. The
[Elastic License 2.0](LICENSE) governs covered code; it does not separately
authorize misleading branding or imply endorsement of a modified build.
ELv2 also requires preservation of the licensor's notices.

## Accurate references

Truthful references in tutorials, reviews, comparisons and integration
documentation are welcome. An unofficial fork may describe its origin as
"based on Citadel" with clear attribution and identification of its independent
maintainer. Do not imply that an unofficial release is maintained or endorsed
by Citadel.

## Modified distributions

Retain required license, copyright and other notices, and clearly identify
modifications as required by LICENSE. Distinct branding does not excuse
removing or obscuring notices protected by ELv2. Seek separate permission
when a proposed branding change requires rights the license does not grant.

Avoid official-looking domains, product listings or logo use that could
mislead users about a distribution's origin or endorsement. This policy does
not add restrictions to LICENSE, override existing permissions, or restrict
lawful nominative/descriptive references or fair use. It makes no claim that
a trademark is registered.

Contact the maintainers through GitHub about uses that could imply official
endorsement. Verify actual trademark ownership and enforceable rights with
counsel before relying on this policy in a dispute.
