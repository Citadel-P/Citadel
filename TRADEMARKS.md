# Citadel name and logo

The Citadel name and logos identify official releases of the Docker
management product. The [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE) grants rights to source code;
it does not separately authorize misleading use of project branding.

## Uses that are welcome

- Truthfully mentioning Citadel in tutorials, reviews, comparisons and
  integration documentation.
- Describing an unofficial fork as "based on Citadel", with clear attribution
  and a clear indication that the fork is independently maintained.
- Redistributing an unmodified Citadel release with accurate provenance,
  subject to applicable license obligations and law.

## Uses that may be misleading

- Branding a modified fork in a way that makes it look like an official
  Citadel release.
- Using the project logo to imply Citadel endorsement or maintenance of
  your fork or commercial offering.
- Using confusing official-looking domains or product listings that could
  misrepresent the origin of a version.

Forks can adopt distinct branding while **retaining copyright, license, and
modification notices required by AGPL-3.0**. This policy does not restrict
software freedoms, lawful nominative/descriptive references, or fair use.
It makes no claim that a trademark is registered.

Contact the project maintainers through GitHub to request permission for a
use that could imply official endorsement. Trademark ownership and any
enforceable rights depend on applicable law and should be verified by counsel.
